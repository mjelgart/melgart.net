## 2. Search bundle (`dist/pagefind/`, 796 K, 30 files)

New in this report. Pagefind is static too — no search server, just an index
fetched a piece at a time. Sorting its output by when a browser actually asks
for it:

| Tier             | Gzipped | Raw     | Files | Trigger |
|------------------|--------:|--------:|------:|---------|
| On page load     |  32.0 K | 131.3 K |     2 | Search island hydrates on `/` |
| On first search  |  95.4 K | 155.7 K |     5 | engine, worker, `wasm.en`, entry/meta |
| Per query        |  92.2 K |  91.8 K |    17 | index + fragment chunks, partial |
| **Never fetched**|       — | **343.4 K** | **6** | nothing on this site references them |

The index and language payloads ship pre-compressed, so the gzipped column
barely moves for them — and for the chunks it reads *larger*, which is gzip
adding framing to data it cannot shrink.

The never-fetched 343 K is `pagefind-component-ui.*` (214 K, a Web Components
UI), `pagefind-modular-ui.*` (22 K, composable primitives), `pagefind-highlight.js`
(44 K, for highlighting terms on click-through destination pages), and
`wasm.unknown.pagefind` (68 K, fallback language index — `pagefind-entry.json`
names only `en`). Pagefind emits its full menu of front-ends on every build
regardless of which one you use; there is no CLI flag to suppress it. Left in
place deliberately: bytes nobody fetches are bytes nobody pays for, and a
cleanup step would silently break the day `pagefind-highlight.js` gets adopted.

### Reading this

**Nothing regressed on the metric that drove the migration.** Post pages still
ship zero JavaScript; the React runtime got marginally smaller across Astro 6 →
7. The pages that grew did so for reasons visible in the diff: dark-mode tokens
and `@font-face` rules are inlined into every document, which is what took a post
page from 4.0 to 6.2 kB gzipped. That is 2.2 kB to never flash white and to set
the site in Literata — a defensible trade, and still 95% below the 136 kB the
Next.js build shipped for the same page.

**The homepage number is the honest correction.** 81.6 kB gzipped is what a
first-time visitor actually downloads, and roughly 76 of those kB are the two
interactive widgets: React (42 kB) and the Pagefind UI (32 kB). Everything this
site does *itself* — every post, every layout, all the content — is the
remaining ~6 kB. The open Preact question in `~/todo.md` is aimed squarely at
the larger of those two.

**`dist/` returning to its Next.js-era 2.6M is not a reversal.** The composition
inverted: baseline was 672 K of framework JS, today it is 160 K of self-hosted
fonts and 932 K of photographs, with `_astro/` holding 172 K of JS for two
islands. Bytes that serve the reader replaced bytes that served the framework.
